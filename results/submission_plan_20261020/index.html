Promotion gates — proposed, not proven thresholds

proposed_practical_threshold: Target ≥0.03 percentage-point mean improvement vs matched local baseline, across at least two development folds and two seeds overall; this is a new decision heuristic, not statistical significance.

component_guardrail: Flag any CD/CV/HEX regression >0.05pp for explicit review; decide using target-level uncertainty and repeatability rather than overall mean alone.

uncertainty: Paired bootstrap over available slide/exact-connected groups; not patient-independent; intervals after model selection are exploratory. Keep all tested arms visible.

package: No missing audit/hash, strict reload and correct pooling, exact preprocessing, allowed hardware/runtime smoke, output contract and checksum. All-data fit has no independent holdout.

fallback: If no candidate clears gates, retain best validated eligible package; do not force novelty or spend both slots.

comparison: Single-model research stays single. Historical uncalibrated/calibrated six are separate comparators. A new ensemble or calibration requires a separately predeclared experiment; not silently mixed into single-model results.
