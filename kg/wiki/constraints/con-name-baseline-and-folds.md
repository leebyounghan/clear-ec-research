# Name the baseline and folds of every delta

## Rule

Before any number goes to the PI: name the baseline and folds of every delta; never call a one- or two-fold result a success; check BRIEF and lane files before saying something was never done.

## Notes

Origin: the coordinator's self-audit, after ViT-L CD -0.036 (against the best pipeline) was quoted as a gain on top of the 6.204 stack.
