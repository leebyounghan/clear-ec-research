# hex6 (six-neighbour share from detector peaks)

HEX read as the share of six-neighbour cells in the Voronoi of detector peaks, from an ensemble of detectors (saturates at about 9). Blended into the HEX prediction (weight 0.28 in the best pipeline; the HEX lane lowered it to 0.16-0.22).
