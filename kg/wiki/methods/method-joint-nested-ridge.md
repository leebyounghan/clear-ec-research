# Joint nested ridge over all HEX stack columns

One ridge on all HEX stack inputs at once (38 or 39 columns) on the log residual of the ap1 base, fitted nested per outer fold, then the stretch; the deploy fit is the same procedure with no outer fold. Replaces the step-by-step HEX chain.
