# External data — historical research only

The September 14 additional SSL stage uses only the 9,000 challenge training
images. External-domain experiments did not supply that release initializer.

The research inventory covered SREP specular endothelium (385 originals,
378 after deduplication), RODREP confocal endothelium (52), a corneal-cell
segmentation repository (160 originals, 157 after deduplication), and
FECD whole-mount microscopy (499 TIFFs). These sources differ in acquisition
modality and must not be treated as one interchangeable specular corpus.

Original source links, sampling policies and research/redistribution restrictions
are retained in the archived inventory. Do not redistribute external images
or infer a new license grant from their inclusion in research records.
The CPSAM foundation checkpoint retains its upstream terms.
