## Defects corrected

1. The previous optimizer omitted the trainable output LayerNorm. Its gradients
   were not cleared by `optimizer.zero_grad()`, while global gradient clipping
   still included them. LayerNorm is now optimized and full parameter coverage
   is asserted. Final five-fold scores show no material baseline gain from this
   correction; see the completion results below.
2. HEX=0 on two rows produced Infinity/NaN in crop summaries and contaminated the
   fitted HEX ridge head. Invalid labels are now masked separately per metric,
   including calibration fitting, and report JSON rejects non-finite values.
3. cover4 previously trained with four tiles but evaluated on five. It now uses
   four tiles for both. D8 orientations are evaluated as separate bags and their
   predictions averaged, rather than treating rotations as additional spatial
   samples inside the mean/std pooling operation.
4. Earlier MIL screens used capped steps and fewer epochs. New MIL/control pairs
   both use 25 complete epochs and batch size 24; MIL costs roughly four times
   the encoder computation while matching image exposures and update count.

GPU smoke tests passed for fixed-epoch held-out extraction and cover4 mean/std
with D8. `code/test_crop_validation.py` passes two regression tests: LayerNorm
updates/gradient clearing, and finite zero-label-safe exploratory/nested reports.

## Measurement caveats

Existing OOF crop summaries are exploratory. Reusing OOF models to cross-fit a
meta-head is not the same as a fully separated base-training/calibration/test
experiment. New five-way rotations use 60% of slides for base training, 20% for
calibration and 20% for testing, with all groups disjoint. They use a fixed last
epoch and training-only target normalization. Prespecified methods are mean,
scalar-only MAPE calibration, quantile aggregation and ridge with alpha=100.
The scalar control tests whether an apparent crop benefit is merely calibration.
Reports include paired slide-cluster bootstrap intervals. These images were used
in earlier investigations, so this is not a historically pristine final test set.

Historical claims that CV/HEX are at a *proven* irreducible noise floor were too
strong. Correlated errors under shared preprocessing and a 25-image ROI test
cannot prove irreducibility. Treat them as an empirical plateau, especially
pending optimizer and crop-pipeline corrections.

## train_green and full-frame data

There are 25 annotated green references, all present in the 8,954-image regression
cache. Their CD/CV/HEX targets match the cache. Their ROI coordinates/markers are
used for diagnosis only; ordinary scalar labels retain the existing training
policy. No ROI-supervised model is trained or evaluated on the same 25 examples.

On these 25 cases central512 retains a mean 68.4% of ROI bounding-box area and
78.8% of marked cell centres; 5 ROI centres lie outside central512. These are
different quantities, and neither establishes generalization across all images.
The references have much higher CV error than the overall OOF set, so they are
not representative enough to set a universal error floor.

`green_eval/mha` provides these 25 full-frame images, but the full-frame cache or
original images for the remaining training set are still absent. Full-frame
weakly supervised ROI/MIL experiments require those originals; additional ROI
GT is not required. The 512 cache cannot reconstruct the missing image periphery.

### Separate train/calibration/test rotations

The five slide-disjoint 60/20/20 rotations gave mean 9.474170, scalar-calibrated
mean 9.245839, quantile 9.258182, and fixed-alpha Ridge 9.216279. Relative to
scalar calibration, Ridge changes error by -0.029559 with paired slide-bootstrap
95% interval [-0.059115, -0.001059] over 6,768 slide groups (2,000 draws).
Ridge wins in three of five rotations. The interval is conditional on the
trained models; it does not measure variation across independent training runs.
This small signal did not replicate as a general Ridge advantage in the ordinary
five-fold OOF comparisons above. Do not automatically promote it to submission.
