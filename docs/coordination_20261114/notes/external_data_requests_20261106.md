## 1. CPTS image set (web form, not email)

Form: https://case.edu/medicine/ophthalmology/research/clinical-research/cornea-preservation-time-study-cpts-public-specular-microscopy-image-set

The page names the form "CPTS Data Acquisition Form". Fields: contact name, email, phone, institution, and the text below.

**Why are you interested in accessing the data?**

> We are taking part in the CLEAR-EC challenge (Corneal Learning for Endothelial Assessment and Review using AI for Endothelial Count), which asks for automated estimates of endothelial cell density, coefficient of variation and hexagonality from eye bank specular images. We would use the CPTS image set for non-commercial research: pretraining and validating image analysis models on donor and postoperative endothelium. We will not redistribute the images, and we will cite the study and follow any terms you set. If reading center measurements (ECD, CV, percent hexagonal cells) can be shared with the images, they would be especially useful. Our challenge deadline is close, so an early reply would be much appreciated.
