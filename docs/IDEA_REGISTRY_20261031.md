`code/eval/eval_oof_20261031.py` (official score, per fold, donor-cluster paired bootstrap vs hybrid B,
slices incl. year and image style). Develop on folds 0-3, confirm once on fold 4. Gains below ~0.02 are not
adopted; anything not validated by 2026-11-09 is out of this challenge. Every submission-2 increment is also reported
on the black-frame stratum (2018/2023/2024 style), where recipe A is weaker than Phase-1.
